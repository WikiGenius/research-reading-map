# Research layers and project relationships

The research question is how motion can help a robot acquire useful information while accomplishing a task. Sensor models and robot constraints vary by application.

| Layer | Public examples | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensing and perception | [Husky image capture](https://github.com/WikiGenius/husky-gazebo-image-capture), [traffic-sign detection](https://github.com/WikiGenius/ros-husky-traffic-sign-detection) | Data acquisition and semantic perception |
| Representation and estimation | [NeRF Lab](https://github.com/WikiGenius/nerf-lab), [GTSAM SLAM Vision](https://github.com/WikiGenius/GTSAM_SLAM_VISION) | Scene representations and pose-estimation experiments |
| Planning and geometry | [WMM planning and tracking](https://github.com/WikiGenius/wmm-planning-and-tracking), [Screw Theory Toolkit](https://github.com/WikiGenius/RoboticScrewTheoryToolkit) | Feasible motion and kinematic reasoning |
| Control and execution | [MoveIt grasping](https://github.com/WikiGenius/ros2-moveit-grasping-demo), [iPendulum](https://github.com/WikiGenius/ipendulum), [Cruise Control](https://github.com/WikiGenius/Cruise_control) | Robot execution and control foundations |

These repositories are separate projects. Links express conceptual relationships, not implemented software dependencies or a validated integrated system.

RGB-D and hyperspectral sensing are initial interests. The [line-scan scaffold](https://github.com/WikiGenius/line-scan-mobile-manipulator-demo) is one planned application, with no complete runnable demo.

The [control index](https://github.com/WikiGenius/robotics-control-learning-labs) and [ROS2 scaffold](https://github.com/WikiGenius/ros2-mobile-robotics-labs) provide navigation and organization. This reading map supplies questions and templates; its paper register is still empty.
